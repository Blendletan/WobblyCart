# Wobbly Cart: submission material draft

2026-11-05. Prepared locally; not sent. The current build is a handling playground. The descriptions below describe the proposed five-trip slice and must not be submitted as present-tense feature claims until those features exist and are checked.

## Pitch

**Wobbly Cart — you only came in for milk.**

A short-session physics skill game about the shopping trolley everyone recognises: four swivelling casters, one troublesome wheel, and a tendency to misbehave at the worst corner. Push it yourself, collect a shopping list, choose between smooth detours and awkward shortcuts, and bring the shopping to checkout. Learn when to slow down, reverse and let the wheels settle. Delivery unlocks the next trip; optional care and speed stamps reward another attempt.

## Intended portal description

You picked the worst trolley in the shop. Bring your groceries to checkout through tight corners, bumps and potholes. Go gently to protect the eggs, or try for a quicker delivery. Every wheel swivels, every turn needs a little judgement, and backing up is sometimes the smartest move.

## Controls

Keyboard: hold W/Up to push, S/Down to pull backwards, A/D or Left/Right to turn, and Space to stop. Release the push to coast. P/Esc pauses. R restarts the trip.
