# H3 playtest — collection or quick packing?

Test the **One more thing** route at <https://blendletan.github.io/WobblyCart/haul.html>. This stage asks one question: is it more enjoyable to collect cargo while driving, or to choose a constrained load before the drive?

## Each tester

1. Play both versions without coaching. Use **Trips** to choose either **3. One more thing** (collection) or **Compare acquisition · One more thing quick pack**.
2. Alternate which version comes first between testers.
3. In quick pack, tap an item then a cart slot, or drag it. Slot 1 is the bottom and slot 4 is the top. Choose **Go** when ready.
4. After the receipt, try **Retry** once to confirm the same load returns. Use **Change load** only if the player wants another arrangement.
5. Allow voluntary replays; do not ask for a fixed number.

Record which version they preferred, why, whether loading felt like a meaningful choice, whether the slot order/risk was understandable, and which version made them voluntarily replay. Also note confusion, accidental starts, and whether losing cargo felt connected to their load or driving choices.

## Decision gate

Stop after the test and choose one of:

- **Collection:** pickups during driving are the stronger game.
- **Packing:** choosing the load before driving adds enough enjoyment to keep.
- **Hybrid:** write one precise rule for what is packed and what is collected; do not carry both complete loops forward by default.

Do not start H4 until that choice is explicit. H3 is a design comparison, not a commitment to freeform 3D packing.
