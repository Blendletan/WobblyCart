# H1: first-haul playtest

Play <https://blendletan.github.io/WobblyCart/haul.html>. Start with **100% wobble / final checkout only**. H2 waits for this feedback.

Try three short attempts:

1. Bring the starting milk and bread to the green checkout. The 25-point goal needs no extra shopping. Hold Push, release to coast, steer, and hold Stop to settle inside checkout.
2. Restart and collect the optional melon by stopping in its circle. Try the rough strip on the right aisle. When it shifts, straighten and brake; on another approach, let it fall and deliver the remainder. Going around the strip is also valid.
3. Open Rules and try Reposition. Remaining goods and elapsed time should survive; the receipt labels this Practice. Optionally compare a different wobble strength or express banking using Apply & restart.

Tell us:

- Could you identify which item was about to fall, and did braking feel like it helped?
- Did losing only the melon make you want to save the rest or immediately restart?
- Was the extra 40 tempting? Did you voluntarily want another attempt?
- Where did steering, instructions or checkout get in the way? What device/browser did you use?

Keyboard: W/Up pushes, S/Down reverses, A/D or Left/Right steers, Space stops, R restarts, Esc/P opens Rules. On-screen controls support holding Push and a steering button together. The timer is informational.

Desktop browser completion and emulated small-screen layouts have been checked. Physical Android/iPhone handling and performance, new-player comprehension and replay appeal remain untested. These player results determine whether to tune H1 or proceed to H2.
