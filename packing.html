How this little experiment works

Tap an object, then tap where it belongs. You can also drag it, rotate its footprint, or park it beside the cart. It settles onto whatever is underneath.

Big, heavy objects on small supports wobble more. Height, overhang and shape matter. The shake repeats exactly so you can compare piles.

For now, lift objects out from the top. Squeezing something underneath a pile and taking this arrangement into the driving game are later experiments.
